The four levels of dress

01

Full formal

Suit and tie, or a structured blazer with a skirt or tailored trousers. Plain fabrics in sober tones, a buttoned collar and leather shoes. When it's cold, a wool overcoat on top.

02

Formal, no tie

The same tailoring without the tie: a jacket over an open-collar shirt, or over a fine knit or sweater. It's a menswear level — in womenswear, these occasions call for full formal or business casual.

03

Business casual

Tailored trousers or a skirt with a shirt, blouse or knit. Vests, cardigans and coats go on top. No tie, and the jacket is optional.

04

Smart casual

A knit, polo or well-cut T-shirt with tailored trousers or dark, straight-leg jeans. Relaxed, for the occasional moment — like happy hour.
